About the workshop

Scaling quantum computing by connecting machines, not just building bigger ones

The path to large-scale quantum computing increasingly relies on interconnecting multiple quantum systems into distributed architectures. This workshop moves from foundations, to the emerging ecosystem, to hands-on experimentation — in three focused sessions.

01

Foundations & challenges

Why DQC matters now: entanglement distribution, cross-node gate execution, latency, compilation and orchestration — and what actually stands in the way of scale.

02

Alliances & ecosystem

How new industry alliances combine quantum hardware, networking, quantum memory and systems expertise on the path toward practical quantum advantage.

03

Sandboxes & experimentation

Experimental platforms that integrate quantum, HPC and generative AI to support end-to-end distributed quantum–classical workflows.

Topics

    Who should attend

    Quantum computing researchers, systems and software engineers, HPC practitioners, application scientists, and industry innovators interested in quantum architectures — along with students and educators from national labs, startups and technology companies. The workshop is highly interactive, mixing invited talks with substantial discussion.
